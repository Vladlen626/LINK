Add a target lock. Q locks onto the nearest slime in a cone in front of the hero (within 10 m); Q again releases it, and Tab switches to the next slime. While locked, the hero faces the target, left/right circle round it and forward/back go toward and away from it, and the camera frames both the hero and the target. The lock drops when the slime is defeated or gets far away (15 m). Keep the locked slime's id in `state.hero.target` (null when nothing is locked).
