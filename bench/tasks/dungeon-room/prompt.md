Build a dungeon room as a new level, `room` (`createGame({ level: 'room' })`, `?level=room` in the page). In it: a block you push by walking into it, onto a floor switch in line with it, which opens a gate; behind the gate a chest with a small key; and a locked door the key opens. Use the kinds `block`, `switch` and `gate` (an open gate has `open: true`); the chest and the locked door are the island's own. The room should remember itself: export `save(state)` (a string) and `load(text)` (a state) from `src/game.js`, and in the page the room is saved as you play and is back as you left it after a reload.
